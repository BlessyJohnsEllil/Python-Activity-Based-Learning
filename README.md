## Case Studies Included

### 1. Case 4 – Employee Payroll and Deduction Analysis
Calculates gross salary, applies progressive deductions, and displays the net salary using arithmetic operations and conditional statements.

### 2. Case 8 – Manufacturing Cost and Profitability Analysis
Calculates total manufacturing cost and profit, then checks whether the specified profit target is achieved.

### 3. Case 10 – Campus Event Financial Planning
Calculates total event expenses, accounts for sponsorship, and determines the minimum number of registrations required to cover the remaining cost.

### 4. Case 11 – Examination Result and Grace-Mark Validation
Validates student marks, applies the specified grace-mark policy, and determines subject-wise and overall examination results.

### 5. Case 12 – Attendance Recovery Computation
Calculates current attendance percentages and simulates the number of future classes required to reach the attendance target under different scenarios.

### 6. Case 16 – Multi-Day Water Tank Simulation
Simulates daily water-level changes, detects overflow and shortage conditions, and identifies the first day the required water-level threshold is reached.

### 7. Case 18 – Fuel Consumption and Route Comparison
Calculates fuel requirements and journey costs for three vehicles, including reserve fuel, and identifies the least expensive option.

### 8. Case 51 – Bus Passenger Occupancy Simulation
Tracks passenger boarding and alighting at multiple stops and checks whether the bus capacity is exceeded.

### 9. Case 53 – Food Waste Monitoring System
Calculates daily food-wastage percentages and identifies whether the wastage exceeds the specified limit.

### 10. Case 63 – Multi-Product Price Fluctuation Analysis
Uses Python lists to calculate product price fluctuations, identify significant changes, and determine the product with the largest percentage fluctuation.
